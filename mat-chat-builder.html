Coach Reminder

Do not announce the lesson too early.

A mat chat lands better when students find the idea before the coach names it. Use the angle as a doorway: tell the setup, ask the question, listen, then give them the word once the meaning has started to appear.

Short is good. Specific is better. Connected to today's class is best.
