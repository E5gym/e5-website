Full Example

Integrity done as discovery, not lecture.

Do not start here

Do not announce the word before students have discovered the idea.

Start concreteImagine you almost landed a skill, but the coach did not see it. You could say you got it and move on, or you could ask for another rep.

Create tensionYou are tired. Class is almost over. And honestly, you did feel it once. Is it really wrong to move on?

AskWhat makes someone choose the harder path even when no one would know?

Student thesis targetThey care about actually being good, not just looking good.

Reveal lateWhat you just described has a name. That is integrity.

Anchor to FireIntegrity is a Fire word because Fire reveals what is underneath. You can look disciplined when someone is watching. Integrity is what remains when they are not.

Close personallyI watched several of you choose the harder rep today when I was not looking. That is already integrity.
