What it is

A real movement class for energetic kids.

NinFit Beginner is E5's foundational weekly class for ages 5-12. Students rotate through parkour, tumbling, trampoline, bars, vaults, landings, and rolls while coaches guide safety, focus, confidence, and character. New students can start when space is available.

How students move

Three training languages. One class rhythm.

Parkour

Jump, land, roll, vault, climb, and move through obstacles with control.

Tumbling

Build safe body shapes, rolling patterns, inversions, and acrobatic foundations.

Trampoline

Train bounce control, timing, aerial awareness, and disciplined excitement.
