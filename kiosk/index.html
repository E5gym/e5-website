Joe

From soldier, to circus performer, to coach, Joe's path shaped the belief behind Elemental 5: movement can build stronger, braver, more capable human beings.

The Short Version

Joe's path moved through military service, circus arts, coaching, travel, and family. Elemental 5 grew out of those experiences and the belief that disciplined movement can shape the whole person.

Why Movement Matters

Joe spent years training, performing, juggling, clowning, and working with children in different parts of the world. That experience taught him that movement is not just exercise. It can build confidence, courage, focus, humor, and resilience.

Why Elemental 5 Exists

Elemental 5 is Joe's attempt to bring those lessons into one clear training environment for kids and families. The goal is not just to teach tricks, but to help students become stronger, more capable, and more self-aware.

What Parents Should Know

Joe believes kids need structure, challenge, play, and coaches who see more than just the skill in front of them. At E5, the movement is the method. Human development is the point.

From M4 to Rubber Chicken is Joe's memoir-in-progress about the strange path from military service to circus life, faith, family, and coaching. That story helps explain the unusual blend of discipline, humor, service, and movement behind Elemental 5.

Elemental Philosophy

The Five Elements

At Elemental 5, the elements give students a language for growth - not just what they are learning, but who they are becoming.

Back to Elements

Earth

Physical Development

Strength, stability, safe landings, body positions, consistency, and foundation work.

Psychological Development

Showing up, listening, patience, responsibility, and learning to do the basics well.

Water

Physical Development

Skill development, coordination, adaptability, mobility, balance, and smooth transitions.

Psychological Development

Flexibility, resilience, emotional control, and learning how to adjust instead of quit.

Fire

Physical Development

Power, speed, explosiveness, commitment, dynamic movement, and courageous effort.

Psychological Development

Confidence, courage, intensity, determination, and the ability to go all in when it matters.

Air

Physical Development

Endurance, rhythm, flow, creativity, expression, and combining skills into movement.

Psychological Development

Communication, imagination, lightness, self-expression, and learning to make movement your own.

Void

Physical Development

Integration, mastery, awareness, timing, and the ability to bring all skills together.

Psychological Development

Presence, focus, humility, leadership, and the ability to stay aware under pressure.
