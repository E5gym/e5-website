New Staff

Welcome Abby Armstrong!

Abby Armstrong — Administrative Manager

Abby has called Salida home for the past five years, working with local nonprofits and in a variety of administrative roles. She’s excited to bring that experience to Elemental 5 as our new Administrative Manager and help keep things running smoothly behind the scenes.

Outside of work, Abby spends most of her time with her family hiking, camping, or hanging by the river. You may also see her son, Royce, around the gym—be sure to say hi to them both!

Founding Membership

Help Build the Next Chapter of E5

As we get closer to opening our new home, we are continuing to invite families, businesses, and community members to become Founding Members of Elemental 5 and directly help finish and equip the new facility.

Founding Membership isn't simply recognition for making a donation. It represents the people who helped turn this facility from an idea into a permanent home for movement, mentorship, training, and community in Chaffee County.

Founding Members will become a permanent part of that story through recognition on the Founders Wall inside the new facility.
