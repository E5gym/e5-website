For Our Families

Quick Reminders for E5 Families

Makeup Tokens

  • A makeup token is issued when a student is marked absent.
  • Makeup tokens do not expire.
  • Tokens can be used while the student is actively enrolled.
  • Makeup classes must be at the same skill level or lower and equal or shorter in duration.
  • Makeup tokens cannot be used for Open Gym.
View Full Gym Policies

Annual Membership / Anniversary Fee

$30 for a single student · $50 for a multi-student family

  • Charged once every 12 months. Taking a break and re-enrolling does not restart the cycle.
  • The multi-student rate applies to multiple student profiles on the same family account, even if only one is enrolled.
  • Waived for active and retired military, firefighters, police, first responders, and teachers. Email gym@elementalfive.com to confirm eligibility before enrollment.
