Parent Reminder

Quick Reminder: Makeup Tokens

  • When a student is marked absent, a makeup token is issued to the account.
  • Makeup tokens do not expire.
  • Tokens can be used for eligible makeup classes while the student is actively enrolled.
  • Makeup classes must be at the same skill level or lower and equal or shorter in duration.
  • Makeup tokens cannot be used for Open Gym.

Parent Reminder

Quick Reminder: Anniversary Fee

  • The annual membership (anniversary) fee is $30 for a single student or $50 for a multi-student family.
  • The multi-student rate applies when multiple student profiles are on one family account, even if only one is enrolled.
  • Once charged, you will not be billed again for 12 months. Taking a break and re-enrolling during that time does not restart the cycle.
  • This fee helps cover facility and equipment costs, insurance, staff background checks, student records, and our iClassPro system.
  • The fee is waived for active and retired military, firefighters, police, first responders, and teachers. Email gym@elementalfive.com to confirm eligibility before enrollment.

New Gym Transition

The Final Stretch

E5 will stay active throughout the fall as we approach the final months in our current gym. Then regular operations will close for the entire month of December.

December will be used to move equipment, finish the build-out, prepare systems, and transition operations into our new home. The projected Grand Opening remains January 2027.
