Founding Membership Campaign

Help build the next E5 home.

Elemental 5 is building a new 6,000 sq ft facility behind our current location with a target opening of Fall 2026. The new gym gives our students a larger training space, a 42x42 spring floor, 25 ft rig points, tramp wall and hot bed, parkour, Elemental Martial Arts, and more room for every E5 program to grow.
