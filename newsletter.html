Important Enrollment Information

The New E5 Is Almost Here.

A New Schedule. A Fresh Start.

All current E5 enrollments will end on November 30. Families do not need to manually cancel their current enrollment for the December transition.

Current Enrollments End November 30

All current E5 enrollments will end on November 30. You do not need to cancel for the move.

No December Tuition

You will not be charged tuition for December. E5 will be closed for the entire month while we move equipment, complete the new facility, prepare systems, train staff, and get the building ready for students.

Re-enrollment Opens in December

When the new schedule is released, every family can choose classes fresh. Current enrollments will not automatically carry over into January.

New Gym = New Schedule

Re-enrollment for the new E5 will begin in December. The new gym means more space, new equipment, expanded programming, and a redesigned schedule. December will be your opportunity to build the right schedule for your family.

  1. November 30

    Current enrollments end.

  2. December

    E5 closed for the move. No December tuition. New schedule released and re-enrollment begins.

  3. January 2–3, 2027

    Grand Opening Weekend.

  4. Early January 2027

    Regular classes begin in the new Elemental 5 facility.
