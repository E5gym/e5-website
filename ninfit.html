What NinFit is

A long-term movement pathway.

NinFit combines parkour, tumbling, and trampoline with intentional coaching and progressive skill development. Kids can start with no experience, build real foundations, and keep growing through Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced levels.

The pathway

Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced. One connected journey.

1-3
Beginner

Build the foundations.

Students learn the basics of safe movement, control, confidence, and class habits.

4-6
Intermediate

Expand your abilities.

Skills become more connected, technical, and intentional as readiness grows.

7-9
Advanced

Pursue mastery.

Advanced athletes chase precision, flow, power, and personal style over time.

Students develop at their own pace. Advancement is based on demonstrated skills, consistency, safety, and readiness, not simply aging into the next group.
