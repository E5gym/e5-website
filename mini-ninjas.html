Strong starts through movement

Why movement matters

In early childhood, trying new ways to move is a powerful way to learn. Mini Ninjas gives children space to explore, practice, and enjoy what their growing bodies can do.

Coordination

Connect movement, timing, and attention through active play.

Balance

Explore stability and control in changing positions and spaces.

Confidence

Build trust in their abilities by trying achievable new challenges.

Body awareness

Notice how the body moves, lands, reaches, and changes direction.

Joyful exploration

Discover the fun of moving, imagining, and learning together.

Play with purpose

What happens in class

E5 coaches lead playful, structured challenges that invite children to explore movement at their own pace. Children participate independently with coaches on the training floor; parents do not join the class on the floor.

  • Climbing
  • Balancing
  • Jumping
  • Rolling
  • Obstacle play
  • Coordination

These are examples of the kinds of movement children may explore, not a fixed checklist or rigid curriculum.

A place to begin, not a deadline

Growing with E5

Mini Ninjas is a standalone early childhood movement program. It offers young children a place to build comfort, coordination, and confidence through movement for its own value.

As children grow, the movement foundations they explore can connect naturally to NinFit. There is no pressure to advance on a timeline; each child can keep learning and enjoying their stage of development.
