What Is Tx3?

Three ways to move.

One integrated class, bringing three movement disciplines together.

One class · Three disciplines
01

Trampoline

Develop aerial awareness, body control, coordination, and confidence through progressive trampoline skills.

02

Tumbling

Build strong acrobatic foundations through rolls, handstands, cartwheels, handsprings, and progressive floor-based movement.

03

Tricking

Explore the creative intersection of martial arts kicks, flips, twists, and dynamic combinations.

The Experience

Build skills.
Find your flow.

Every new skill begins with a foundation. Tx3 challenges students to develop strength, coordination, spatial awareness, and control while exploring increasingly dynamic movement.

It's a place to work toward exciting physical goals, celebrate progress, and discover what the body can do.
