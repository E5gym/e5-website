Mat Chat Word Bank

The 24 Mat Chat Words

These are the core Elemental 5 words used for mat chats. Some are virtues. Some are habits, training concepts, physical practices, or mindset qualities. All of them help students connect movement training to character development.

Earth / Foundation

Words that build the base.

Earth words help students show up, own their choices, care for their body, and become dependable members of the room.

Discipline

Kid-friendly meaning

Doing the right thing over and over, even when it is not exciting.

Training connection

Repeating clean basics before chasing the bigger skill.

Mat chat angle

Start with a student who wants to skip the setup, then help the group discover why the small reps matter.

Open Discipline Prompt

Fitness

Kid-friendly meaning

Building a body that is ready to move, work, and recover.

Training connection

Finishing conditioning with quality even when the fun skill work is over.

Mat chat angle

Start with the last hard round of warmups and ask why athletes care about strength when no trick is happening.

Open Fitness Prompt

Responsibility

Kid-friendly meaning

Owning your choices and helping take care of the room.

Training connection

Resetting a mat, fixing spacing, or owning a mistake without blaming someone else.

Mat chat angle

Start with a station left unsafe and let students find why everyone shares the room.

Open Responsibility Prompt

Nutrition

Kid-friendly meaning

Giving your body the fuel, water, and care it needs to train well.

Training connection

Noticing how energy, focus, and safety change when the body is cared for.

Mat chat angle

Start with a student trying to train on low energy and ask what the body needs before class.

Open Nutrition Prompt

Integrity

Kid-friendly meaning

Being truthful about your work, even when nobody would know.

Training connection

Asking for another rep instead of claiming a skill that was not really clean.

Mat chat angle

Start with a student who almost landed something when the coach did not see, then let the group name why truth matters.

Open Integrity Prompt

Community

Kid-friendly meaning

Being part of a group where everyone helps the room get better.

Training connection

Sharing stations, cheering teammates, cleaning up, and making space.

Mat chat angle

Start with a busy open gym moment and ask what happens when athletes care about more than their own turn.

Open Community Prompt

Water / Skill + Adaptability

Words that help students respond.

Water words help students receive coaching, adjust to change, support others, and stay flexible when training gets hard.

Respect

Kid-friendly meaning

Treating people, equipment, and coaching like they matter.

Training connection

Stopping to receive a correction before taking a turn.

Mat chat angle

Start with a student eager to go while the coach is giving one safety cue.

Open Respect Prompt

Humility

Kid-friendly meaning

Staying teachable, even when you are already good.

Training connection

Taking a basic correction seriously instead of acting above it.

Mat chat angle

Start with an advanced student asked to return to a simple drill.

Open Humility Prompt

Kindness

Kid-friendly meaning

Using your words and actions to help someone feel safe enough to keep trying.

Training connection

Encouraging a teammate after missed reps or nervous attempts.

Mat chat angle

Start with a student embarrassed after several misses and let the group decide what helps.

Open Kindness Prompt

Self-Control

Kid-friendly meaning

Being in charge of your body and choices when your energy is big.

Training connection

Holding trampoline energy in line and using it safely on your turn.

Mat chat angle

Start with a student excited enough to bounce, talk, and rush before their turn.

Open Self-Control Prompt

Empathy

Kid-friendly meaning

Remembering what a moment might feel like for someone else.

Training connection

Supporting a newer or nervous student instead of judging them.

Mat chat angle

Start with a skill that feels easy now but used to feel scary.

Open Empathy Prompt

Adaptability

Kid-friendly meaning

Changing your plan when the first plan does not work.

Training connection

Adjusting a parkour line, station, or correction instead of getting stuck.

Mat chat angle

Start with a familiar setup changing and ask what helps an athlete keep moving.

Open Adaptability Prompt

Fire / Power + Drive

Words that move energy into action.

Fire words help students face pressure, aim effort, take safe risks, and bring power with purpose.

Courage

Kid-friendly meaning

Doing the right thing even when part of you feels nervous.

Training connection

Trying a safe coached skill attempt even when your body feels scared.

Mat chat angle

Do not start by saying the word. Start with a scenario where a student can step back or take a safe coached attempt.

Open Courage Prompt

Confidence

Kid-friendly meaning

Trusting your preparation enough to try.

Training connection

Taking a supported next step after drilling the prep work.

Mat chat angle

Start with a student who has prepared but still does not feel completely ready.

Open Confidence Prompt

Determination

Kid-friendly meaning

Keeping your effort pointed at the work when it gets hard.

Training connection

Missing a skill, resetting, and taking one better rep.

Mat chat angle

Start with a student stuck on the same correction for several attempts.

Open Determination Prompt

Goal Setting

Kid-friendly meaning

Choosing a clear target so your effort knows where to go.

Training connection

Picking one skill or correction during open gym instead of wandering.

Mat chat angle

Start with a student bouncing between every station and improving very little.

Open Goal Setting Prompt

Focus

Kid-friendly meaning

Putting your attention where it belongs right now.

Training connection

Holding one coach cue in your mind for the next rep.

Mat chat angle

Start with a noisy line and one important correction before a turn.

Open Focus Prompt

Passion

Kid-friendly meaning

Loving something enough to bring energy and care to it.

Training connection

Using excitement for aerial, trampoline, or parkour without turning it into chaos.

Mat chat angle

Start with a student who loves a skill so much they want to rush everything.

Open Passion Prompt

Air / Awareness + Mindset

Words that open awareness.

Air words help students breathe, notice, learn, wait, appreciate, and stay light enough to keep growing.

Perseverance

Kid-friendly meaning

Returning to a hard thing long enough to grow.

Training connection

Working the same skill across weeks without avoiding it.

Mat chat angle

Start with a student who is better than before but still not where they want to be.

Open Perseverance Prompt

Patience

Kid-friendly meaning

Giving the process enough time to work.

Training connection

Respecting prep skills before moving to the next aerial wrap, flip, or vault.

Mat chat angle

Start with a student who wants the advanced version before the foundation is ready.

Open Patience Prompt

Knowledge

Kid-friendly meaning

Understanding why something works, not just copying it.

Training connection

Knowing why a cue, body shape, or safety rule matters.

Mat chat angle

Start with a student who copies a shape but does not understand why the detail matters.

Open Knowledge Prompt

Mindfulness

Kid-friendly meaning

Noticing what is happening in your body and mind before you react.

Training connection

Taking one breath after a missed rep before trying again.

Mat chat angle

Start with a student who wants to rush the next rep to erase a mistake.

Open Mindfulness Prompt

Gratitude

Kid-friendly meaning

Noticing and appreciating what helps you train.

Training connection

Thanking spotters, coaches, teammates, and the shared space.

Mat chat angle

Start with all the invisible help that makes one athlete's turn possible.

Open Gratitude Prompt

Playfulness

Kid-friendly meaning

Staying curious and light while still being safe.

Training connection

Exploring a parkour line creatively without becoming careless.

Mat chat angle

Start with a student getting stiff because they are trying too hard to be perfect.

Open Playfulness Prompt
