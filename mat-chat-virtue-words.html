The Words

The 24 Virtue Words

These are the core character words used in E5 mat chats. Each word should be connected to training, not treated like a vocabulary lesson.

Earth

Structure

Earth words build the foundation. They teach students how to show up, tell the truth, do the work, and become dependable.

Honesty

Definition: Telling the truth about what happened.

Training connection: Where did honesty show up in class today?

Responsibility

Definition: Owning your choices instead of blaming someone else.

Training connection: What does it look like to own your training today?

Patience

Definition: Staying calm when progress takes longer than you want.

Training connection: What skill is teaching you patience right now?

Reliability

Definition: Being someone others can count on.

Training connection: How can your group count on you today?

Respect

Definition: Treating people, space, and equipment with care.

Training connection: What does respect look like at this station?

Commitment

Definition: Staying with what you said you would do.

Training connection: What are you committed to improving today?

Water

Adaptability

Water words teach students how to adapt, stay coachable, work with others, and move through difficulty without hardening.

Empathy

Definition: Remembering what something feels like for someone else.

Training connection: How can you support someone who is struggling?

Humility

Definition: Staying teachable even when you are good.

Training connection: What correction can you receive today?

Forgiveness

Definition: Letting a mistake become a lesson instead of a label.

Training connection: How can you reset after a rough rep?

Openness

Definition: Being willing to try a new approach.

Training connection: What new cue are you willing to test?

Cooperation

Definition: Working with others to make the group better.

Training connection: How can you help your station run well?

Compassion

Definition: Responding to someone with care instead of judgment.

Training connection: Who needs encouragement instead of pressure?

Fire

Presence

Fire words teach students how to bring energy, take action, face fear, and commit fully.

Courage

Definition: Doing something even when you feel nervous.

Training connection: Where did courage show up in class today?

Effort

Definition: Giving real work to the rep in front of you.

Training connection: Where can you add effort without adding chaos?

Intensity

Definition: Bringing strong energy with control.

Training connection: When does this skill need more intensity?

Confidence

Definition: Trusting your preparation enough to try.

Training connection: What part of the skill can you trust today?

Action

Definition: Moving from thinking into doing.

Training connection: What is the next right rep?

Determination

Definition: Continuing when the work gets hard.

Training connection: What will you keep working on after it gets frustrating?

Air

Awareness

Air words teach students how to pay attention, see clearly, communicate, and make better choices.

Focus

Definition: Putting your attention where it belongs.

Training connection: What is one thing you need to pay attention to today?

Listening

Definition: Taking in information before responding.

Training connection: What cue do you need to hear before your next rep?

Communication

Definition: Using words and signals that help the group.

Training connection: What do you need to say clearly today?

Timing

Definition: Knowing when to move, wait, or adjust.

Training connection: Where does timing matter in this skill?

Perspective

Definition: Seeing the moment from more than one angle.

Training connection: What could you notice differently today?

Creativity

Definition: Finding a useful new way to solve a problem.

Training connection: How can you solve this challenge safely?

Void

Integration

Void words are advanced. They teach students how to bring everything together and stay present under pressure.

Mastery

Definition: Bringing skill, control, awareness, and calm together.

Training connection: What does mastery look like before a skill is advanced?

Presence

Definition: Being fully here for the moment you are in.

Training connection: What helps you stay present before a hard rep?

Self-Control

Definition: Choosing your response instead of being ruled by impulse.

Training connection: Where do you need control more than power?

Calm Under Pressure

Definition: Staying steady when the moment feels intense.

Training connection: What can you do when pressure rises?

Awareness

Definition: Noticing yourself, the room, and the task at the same time.

Training connection: What should you notice before you move?

Integration

Definition: Putting separate lessons together in one rep.

Training connection: What do you need to combine today?
