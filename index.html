The E5 System

Not Just a Gym.
A Pathway.

Elemental 5 trains students across acrobatics, aerial arts, martial arts, and fitness through a structured progression system. The goal is not just better movement; it is the formation of capable bodies, confident minds, and humble character.

We call this student the Elemental Athlete.

Earth

Strength · Stability · Foundation

Students build the physical and mental base to show up, listen, work hard, and move with control.

Water

Skill · Adaptability · Flow

Students learn technical progressions, adjust when skills are difficult, and develop coordination across disciplines.

Fire

Power · Courage · Commitment

Students learn to bring full effort to hard skills, scary moments, and meaningful challenges.

Air

Endurance · Awareness · Expression

Students build stamina, creativity, and the ability to move with rhythm, style, and presence.

Void

Integration · Mastery · Character

Students learn to connect the elements, becoming more focused, capable, respectful, and self-directed.

Every class, skill, and level at E5 is part of this larger pathway.

Parkour · Tumbling · Ninja Training

The flagship E5 program. A 9-level progression system that builds real movement skills alongside the grit, courage, and focus to use them. Kids earn levels, not just stickers.

  • Tumbling and acrobatic foundations

  • Character development built into every class

Tx3

Trampoline · Tumbling · Tricking

For kids who can't get enough flips. Tx3 focuses on trampoline skills, tumbling progressions, and tricking: the art of combining acrobatics with style and creativity.

  • Trampoline skills and routine development
  • Tumbling from basics to advanced
  • Tricking: kicks, flips, and combos
  • 5-level progression system

Ages 8+

Aerial Silks

Grace · Strength · Expression

Soar above the rest. Aerial silks builds extraordinary upper-body strength, spatial awareness, and artistic expression while learning to move beautifully through the air.

  • Climbs, wraps, and inversions
  • Drops and dynamic movement
  • Choreography and performance skills
  • Strength conditioning built in

Ages 3–5

Mini Ninjas

Movement · Play · Foundation

Where it all begins. Mini Ninjas introduces the youngest movers to jumping, rolling, climbing, and balancing through play-based movement. Big confidence built in small bodies.

  • Fundamental movement and body awareness
  • Introductory tumbling and jumping skills
  • Obstacle courses and guided play
  • Character seeds planted from day one
