How E5 works

Grow strong. Find your people.

E5 classes are built around purposeful progress. Students learn real skills, practice safely, and develop the character to keep showing up when the work gets difficult.

01

Train the body

Strength, mobility, coordination, balance, power, and control are developed through clear progressions.

02

Build the skill

Students learn movement patterns in steps, with coaches guiding technique, effort, and readiness.

03

Shape the person

Character is not a side lesson. It is part of how students listen, try, lead, recover, and encourage others.

The five elements

MORE THAN MOVEMENT.

At Elemental 5, we use five elements to teach more than physical skills. We help students develop strength, adaptability, courage, creativity, and an understanding of who they are becoming.

Earth

Build your body

Strength, structure, stability, and the base that makes every skill safer.

Water

Learn to move

Flow, coordination, parkour, tumbling, and the ability to adapt.

Fire

Learn to act

Commitment, courage, intensity, and purposeful action under pressure.

Air

Learn to express

Circus, aerial, performance, timing, creativity, and presence.

Void

Understand who you are becoming

Void is not a fifth discipline. It is where training comes together: self-awareness, leadership, character, and the meaning behind the work.
