The 4-Step Formula

Keep it simple enough to use.

1

Name the idea

"Today we're talking about courage."

2

Connect it to training

"Courage shows up when you try something even though you feel nervous."

3

Ask one question

"Where might you need courage today?"

4

Give a challenge

"When something feels hard today, take one breath and try again."

What actually happened in class today that connects to this word?

What do I want students to notice?

What question will make them think?

What simple challenge can they carry back into training?

Age Adjustments

Match the mat chat to the student.

Mini Ninjas / ages 3-5

Use one sentence, one image, one action.

Ages 5-8

Use a short story or concrete example.

Ages 9-12

Ask a question and let them answer.

Teens

Make it more honest, direct, and responsibility-based.

Coach Reminders

Say less. Connect more.

A mat chat works when students can carry it back into movement immediately.

  • Keep it under 3 minutes.
  • Do not lecture.
  • Ask one good question.
  • Connect the idea to what students are about to do.
  • End with a simple challenge.
  • Bring it back later during class.

Element Prompt Cards

Use the elements to shape the conversation.

Earth Structure

Words: honesty, responsibility, patience, reliability, respect, commitment

Earth mat chats help students connect character to consistency, boundaries, and trust.

Sample question: What choice helps the whole group feel safe and ready today?

Water Adaptability

Words: empathy, humility, forgiveness, openness, cooperation, compassion

Water mat chats help students notice how they respond when the moment changes.

Sample question: How can you adjust if your first plan does not work?

Fire Presence

Words: courage, effort, intensity, confidence, commitment, action

Fire mat chats turn energy into brave, focused action instead of noise.

Sample question: Where do you need to bring full effort today?

Air Awareness

Words: focus, listening, communication, timing, perspective, creativity

Air mat chats help students see, listen, time, and communicate more clearly.

Sample question: What will you pay attention to before you move?

Void Integration

Words: mastery, presence, calm under pressure, awareness, self-control, putting it all together

Void mat chats help students bring multiple lessons together in real time.

Sample question: What do you need to combine to make this rep better?
