How to lead a warm-up

Start with presence, clear boundaries, simple progressions, and visible expectations. Keep it moving and connect it to the class goal.

How to run stations

Name the station, show the rep, define the wait spot, and rotate before the room gets stale.

How to redirect a student

Use calm, specific language: name the behavior, name the expectation, give a next action.

How to manage group energy

Raise structure when energy rises. Lower complexity when focus drops. Use transitions before chaos builds.

When class gets chaotic

Pause the room, gather students, reset the safety boundary, simplify the task, and restart with one clear standard.

What to say

  • Scared: We can scale it. Your next brave step is smaller than the whole skill.
  • Frustrated: Take one breath. We are looking for one better rep.
  • Distracted: Eyes here, body still, next job is this station.
  • Overexcited: Keep the energy, add control.
