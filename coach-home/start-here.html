What this Coach Home is for

A practical internal resource for E5 coaches before stepping onto the floor. Use it to review standards, language, class systems, skill cues, mat chat ideas, and the elemental coaching framework.

What it is not for

It is not a replacement for mentorship, staff training, in-person feedback, or the repetitions that turn knowledge into leadership.

The E5 Coaching Standard

We coach with safety, clarity, energy, respect, and purpose. We build movement skills and human skills at the same time.

How to use this section

Scan before class, revisit after hard coaching moments, and bring questions back to lead coaches. The best use is short, repeated, and connected to what happens on the floor.

Coach development happens on the floor.

This site supports coaching development. It does not replace mentorship, feedback, repetition, or real-time leadership on the floor.
