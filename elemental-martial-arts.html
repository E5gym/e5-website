What We Train

Traditional roots. Practical training.

Elemental Martial Arts draws from Japanese Budo Taijutsu (Ninjutsu), Jujutsu, Judo, and Aikijutsu, alongside Karate, Kickboxing, Wrestling, and modern self-defense systems including Krav Maga.

These disciplines contribute striking, throws, takedowns, standing and ground grappling, escapes, and practical defensive skills. Rather than training each style separately, EMA brings their techniques and principles together into one integrated program.
